# Piece set: cburnett

These are the chess pieces lichess uses by default. They were drawn by
[Colin M.L. Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett), and the copies here come from
[lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett).

Lichess distributes them under GPLv2+. The original drawings on
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces) are offered under
GFDL, BSD, GPL or CC BY-SA 3.0, at your choice.
